Cambridge Assessment English (CAE)

CAE is a part of Cambridge University Press & Assessment, a department of the University of Cambridge, offering a world-renowned approach to teaching, learning, and assessing English.

CAE believes English unlocks a lifetime of opportunities. Through inclusive, research-based learning and assessment experiences, we help people learn and confidently prove their English skills.

Backed by academic excellence and global insights, CAE's programs meet real-world needs — enabling learners to connect, communicate, and succeed worldwide.

CAE's global reach is over 2,800 authorised exam centres, 50,000+ preparation centres, and a presence in 130 countries with 27 international offices.

CAE collaborates with educators and institutions to ensure high-quality, accessible English learning experiences for everyone.

Cambridge English Exam Centre

Carbonblue is an authorised Cambridge English Language Assessment centre in India, with centre number IA612. Carbonblue is one of the leading Cambridge English Exams centres in India.

We specialize in preparing candidates for Cambridge English’s proficiency examinations, equipping them with globally recognized qualifications aligned with the CEFR (Common European Framework of Reference for Languages), UK.

Our training approach is both structured and personalized—meticulously designed to accommodate diverse learning styles and individual learner needs.

We continuously strive to align our methodologies with current best practices, ensuring our learners receive the most effective and up-to-date instruction.
